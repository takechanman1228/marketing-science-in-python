# Chapter 5.3 seed — synthetic weekly sales (author-generated)

Committed seeds for *5.3 Sales Forecasting and Scenario Planning*:

| file | rows | contents |
|---|---|---|
| `weekly_sales.csv` | 312 | Six exact 52-week fiscal years (Mondays 2014-01-06 … 2019-12-23) of weekly e-commerce revenue with `holiday_week` / `bau_promotion` flags. What the analyst sees. |
| `campaign_effect_estimate.csv` | 3 | The "measured" scenario increments (point estimate + uncertainty, as a geo experiment or calibrated MMM would deliver them). |
| `ground_truth.csv` | 312 | The generator's noise-free expected sales and the true campaign counterfactual. Never used to fit or tune the chapter's models; read only by the notebook's closing section and the tests. |

**Provenance.** Author-generated by
`notebooks/part5-commercial-analytics/sales_forecast_data_generation.py`
(`generate_dataset(seed=42)`); regenerating must reproduce these files exactly —
the test suite treats any drift as an RNG/logic regression. The generator's
docstring records why the chapter uses synthetic data and which design
parameters are frozen.

**Calendar.** Fiscal years are fixed 52-week blocks that close in late
December; Christmas sits in one of each block's final two weeks (fiscal week
51 for FY2014–16, week 52 for FY2017–19, because a real year is slightly
longer than 52 weeks). Holiday effects (Black Friday, Cyber week, gift rush,
the post-cutoff drop) are located from the real calendar, and their strength
varies by year; the true effect of the FY2019 "additional holiday campaign"
scenario is +7% on fiscal weeks 47–50 (the shipped estimate is deliberately
noisier: 0.08 ± 0.025).
